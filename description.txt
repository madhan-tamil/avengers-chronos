# Avengers: Project CHRONOS — The Last Five Seconds

**Category:** Web Exploitation  
**Difficulty:** Intermediate–Advanced

### Mission Brief

Following the events of the Battle of Earth, S.H.I.E.L.D. recovered a classified temporal archive developed using Stark Industries technology. The archive, known as **Project CHRONOS**, was designed to maintain records of authorized time-travel operations.

Something has gone wrong.

The archive's interface is unstable, its diagnostic messages reference a narrow synchronization window, and its incident logs contain conflicting timestamps. Attempts to access the classified mission records lead nowhere.

Your mission is to investigate the anomalous interface, uncover the clues hidden within the archive, reconstruct the relevant timeline, and find a way to recover the sealed mission record.

Every second matters. The truth is buried somewhere between the requests, the records, and the last five seconds.

**Can you restore the timeline and recover the classified flag?**
